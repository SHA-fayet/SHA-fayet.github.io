Agricultural AI Analysis

Q1 JOURNALS SUBMITTED

My culminating thesis focuses on the intersection of agriculture and artificial intelligence. I designed a novel Deep Learning model specifically engineered to classify mango leaf morphology.

By analyzing the visual data of over 11,200 leaves through a Triensemble Framework (CNN, ViT, DenseNet) and Computer Vision techniques, the system achieved 97.57% accuracy on multiclass classification and 99.00% accuracy on binary prediction—detecting exact fruit varieties and predicting pre-harvest sweetness/sourness profiles.
